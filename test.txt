     \[
     \mathcal L(\theta)
     =

     \frac{1}{M}
     \sum_{m=1}^{M}\mathcal L_m(\theta)
     \]

     and JAX differentiates through each seven-day simulation:

     \[
     \nabla_\theta\mathcal L
     \]

     Parameter Bounds

     JEM-Cal optimizes unconstrained parameters \(u\), then maps them smoothly into physical ranges. For a bounded parameter:

     \[
     \theta =
     \theta_{\min}
     +
     (\theta_{\max}-\theta_{\min})\sigma(u)
     \]
